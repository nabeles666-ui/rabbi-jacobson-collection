2015-01-15 · 19.7 minutes · Source: 20150115 125329.txt

Continuing a graded discussion of falsehood, the lecture examines claiming virtues one does not possess and repeating stories without sufficient care. It distinguishes deliberate invention from less direct forms of misleading speech.
